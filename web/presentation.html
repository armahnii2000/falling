Key Theoretical Finding

The $+5\text{ PSI}$ vs. $-30\text{ PSI}$ Asymmetry

In our reversed defusal mechanics, victory and defeat have wildly unequal buffer margins:

Defeat Buffer (Meltdown Headroom)
Starting 30.0 PSI → 35.0 PSI Rupture
+5.0 PSI (Razor Thin!)
Victory Distance (Defusal Target)
Starting 30.0 PSI → 0.0 PSI Stabilized
-30.0 PSI (6× Larger Gap!)
💡 Why 1:1 Ratios Break: Because the rupture buffer is $6\times$ narrower than the defusal target, a symmetric 50/50 or 40/40 ratio results in a 74%–93% blowout rate. Hot blocks must always remain a strictly controlled minority.

Next Evolution: Dynamic Adaptive Ratios (DDA)

Instead of keeping the spawn ratio static throughout the whole match, the engine can adaptively shift based on current reactor pressure:

State A: Near-Meltdown Emergency (Pressure ≥ 31.0 PSI)
Shift to Emergency Coolant Reserve: 1 Hot : 6 Cold : 3 Norm

Prevents arbitrary instant loss on bad RNG rolls, giving the player an active lifeline to claw their way back.

Clutch Recovery
State B: Nominal Operating Zone (12.0 – 30.0 PSI)
Maintain Standard Arcade Defusal: 2 Hot : 4 Cold : 4 Norm

Natural flow state. Regular stacking, push-out cascades, and steady depressurization.

Core Gameplay
State C: Final Depressurization Sprint (Pressure ≤ 8.0 PSI)
Shift to Resistance Heat Surge: 3 Hot : 4 Cold : 3 Norm

As you close in on victory, the core violently vents thermal pockets, preventing a boring runaway cruise to the finish line.

Climactic Finish
Summary: Static 2:4:4 delivers an excellent baseline. Adaptive DDA elevates it into an elastic, dramatic narrative arc.
