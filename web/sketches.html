Mathematical Fact: Exactly 3 Pyramids Form 1 Cube!

You asked: "Does a cube divide into pyramids?"
YES! A cube divides into exactly THREE identical square-based pyramids that join at a common corner.

  • Euclid's Classic Proof: Each pyramid has a square base ($s \times s$) and height $s$. Volume of each is $\frac{1}{3} s^3$. Three of them together make $\frac{3}{3} s^3 = s^3$ (the full cube!).

Option A · Physical Balancing Act

1. The 3-Legged Balance Table (RGB Stewart Platform)

A physical circular platform resting on three hydraulic legs (Red, Green, Blue at 120° angles). On top of the table rests a delicate, glowing white core orb. The heights of the three legs respond directly to accumulated or stacked RGB values.

Core Mechanics & Penalties

  • Equal 1:1:1 Distribution: The platform is perfectly flat (0° tilt); the white orb stays balanced in the dead center.
  • Imbalance Tilt: If Red accumulates to 18 while Blue is only 3, the Red leg pumps up, tipping the table toward the lower legs.
  • The Roll-Off Penalty: If tilt passes a critical threshold (>25°), the white core rolls downhill. If it falls off the edge into the hazard zone, you trigger a catastrophic collapse (lose lives or penalty).
  • High-Stakes Decision Making: You can no longer mindlessly farm high-tier Red merges. You must actively hunt for Blue and Green to stabilize the table before taking another shot!

4. Building a Pyramid From Pyramids (Hierarchical Assembly)

Can you build a pyramid out of smaller pyramids? Yes! Both in 2D (Triangles forming larger Triangles) and in 3D (Hierarchical Pyramids forming a Megalith).

Mathematical & Gameplay Models

  • Stepped Pyramid Stacking: 1 peak pyramid rests in the saddle of 4 supporting pyramids below it (2×2), which in turn rest on 9 pyramids (3×3), totaling 14 pyramids to form a 3-tier monument.
  • 2D Equilateral Triforce Rule: 4 smaller triangles (3 pointing up ▲, 1 inverted ▼ in center) assemble into a 2× scale triangle without any empty space.
  • 3D Sierpinski Tetrahedron: 4 corner tetrahedra create a double-sized tetrahedron with an octahedral energy core cavity in the center!
  • Balanced Construction: If you build up one side of the pyramid too fast with Red bricks, the center of mass moves off-axis, causing the pyramid to shear and tumble!
