Into the Darkness — 3-Layer System

Separating the experience into 3 distinct modular layers: the protagonist's smartphone underneath, a frosted translucent glass chamber plate in the middle, and the transparent 3D block game board on top.

🎨 3 Non-Neon Themes 🌑 Play Darkness (Mobile + Themes) 🖥️ 16:9 Desktop View 📐 Proportions Chart
Layer 1: Phone Screen
Base Layer

Leo's personal smartphone lockscreen at 3:32 AM with 14% low battery (red). Incoming urgent text messages from Wife ("Leo, please come to bed...") and corporate Slack revoke alerts.

Layer 2: Frosted Glass
Translucent Filter

A smoked translucent glass sheet with beveled crystal edge rim-lighting. Applies diffuse refraction and soft Gaussian blur to the phone screen underneath without hiding it.

Layer 3: Game Board
Alpha Transparent

100% transparent background. Only the game UI lives here: top score & tier HUD, dropper slider rail, glowing cube, laser guide line, 3D square blocks (1x1 to black cube), and SWAP button.
