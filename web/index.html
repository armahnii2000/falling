Gameplay Dynamics: n×n Grid vs Circles

🟦 What changes with n×n Squares?

  • No Roll-Off: Circles slide down crevices. Squares have flat horizontal and vertical faces that stack into columns and bridges.
  • Push-Out Expansion: Two $n \times n$ squares merge into an $(n+1) \times (n+1)$ square. Because $n+1 > n$, the new square is wider and taller, forcefully pushing adjacent stacks outward and downward!
  • Size 12 Singularity: Reaching the $12 \times 12$ square (144 cells, ~75% of chamber width) triggers an epic fanfare and disappears, causing all buoyant blocks below to surge upward!

🎮 Controls

Desktop: Use A / D or Arrow keys to aim, and Space / W to launch. Drag mouse directly on the grid or slider to aim.

Mobile: Drag along the slider track or touch the chamber to aim, then tap LAUNCH.
